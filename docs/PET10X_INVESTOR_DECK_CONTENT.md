## Before presenting

Use these labels consistently:

- **IN PRODUCT** — implemented in the current application code.
- **LIMITED / MANUAL** — implemented, but missing an integration or important
  production control.
- **EXPANSION** — a market or capability thesis; not yet built.
- **PRICING HYPOTHESIS** — a price shown in product or planning material, but
  not yet supported by live billing and validated retention data.

Do not call seeded demo accounts, pets, buildings, bookings, or incidents
"traction." Replace every bracketed field in Slides 21 and 23 with verified business

## Slide 3 — The problem

### Pet ownership is growing. The operating infrastructure around it is fragmented.

- Owners keep care, vaccination, licence, insurance, and emergency details in
  different apps, inboxes, paper files, and memory.
- Property managers use spreadsheets, email, and ad hoc complaint handling for
  a process that may end in formal warnings, hearings, fines, or disputes.
- Municipal reporting often begins with the resident finding the right
  jurisdiction, phone number, and form on their own.
- Clinics hold the most trusted health records, but owners repeatedly upload or
  retype those records for buildings and service providers.
- Local pet businesses buy broad advertising even when the relevant demand is
  concentrated inside nearby buildings.

**Speaker note**

> Fragmentation is expensive because the same fact is repeatedly collected and
> repeatedly distrusted. "Is this vaccination current?" is answered once by an
> owner, again by a vet, again by a building, and sometimes again by a city or
> insurer. Pet10x turns those repeated handoffs into a network.

**Suggested visual:** Five screenshots or document icons with broken arrows
between them; one duplicated vaccination certificate appears in every system.

### Buildings create distribution, urgency, and local network density at the same time.

- One property relationship can introduce Pet10x to dozens or hundreds of

- A building is a naturally verified local community, improving trust in posts,
  lost-and-found, events, and service discovery.
- Pet owners can still use Pet10x without a building for profiles and care;

## Slide 7 — The owner journey

### From first profile to a reusable pet record

1. **Create an account** with email verification.
2. **Join a property** with a building code—or continue as a standalone owner.
3. **Create pet profiles** with photos, records, contacts, and care needs.
4. **See what is missing** for the household and the building.
5. **Follow daily care** and receive in-app care/vaccination reminders.
6. **Read rules and participate** in the private building community.
7. **Find and book** a nearby vet, groomer, walker, trainer, boarding provider,

8. **Handle exceptional moments**—incident, dispute, accommodation, lost pet,
   or emergency information—from the same identity.

## Slide 9 — The strongest workflow

### A complaint becomes a traceable resolution—not another email thread

**Report** → **Evidence** → **Manager triage** → **Violation** → **Warning** →
**Fine 1** → **Fine 2** → **Resolved or dismissed**

At every stage:

- The subject is the identified pet owner, not the reporter.
- Photos can be uploaded before account creation and are claimed by the report.
- Managers can resolve or dismiss at any active stage.
- Open disputes pause further enforcement.
- A resident can appeal within the defined window.
- Manager decisions and fine reminders create a durable event trail.

**Status:** **IN PRODUCT** and database-guarded for the core violation and
dispute transitions. Card payment and some surrounding incident-state controls
remain roadmap work.

**Speaker note**

> This workflow demonstrates the product's depth. The business value is not the
> button that says "fine"; it is the controlled sequence, the evidence, the
> resident's right to respond, and the record of who did what.

**Suggested visual:** Use the flow above as a large timeline, with a branch from
Fine to Dispute → Upheld / Overturned.

## Slide 12 — Government and municipalities

### A structured front door for animal-control and bylaw workflows

**What exists today — LIMITED / MANUAL**

- Public or signed-in municipal report intake for attacks, threatening animals,
  and animals at large
- Postal-code jurisdiction matching, optional GPS/location capture, a Pet10x
  reference number, and the municipality's official contact
- Clear disclosure that Pet10x records the report but does **not** forward it
  to authorities

**Institutional expansion**

- Direct case-routing API into municipal animal-control or 311 systems
- Municipality-maintained licence requirements and renewal validation
- Structured evidence, duplicate detection, status feedback, and case closure
- Privacy-preserving trend dashboards for hotspots, repeat incidents, licence
  gaps, and service planning
- Optional emergency-readiness collaboration, explicitly supplementary and not
  a replacement for standard response protocols

**Buyer:** Municipal animal services, bylaw departments, digital-service teams,
and regional authorities.

**Revenue path:** Annual platform contract + implementation/integration fees;
aggregate analytics only under an approved privacy and data-sharing framework.

**Status:** Government connectivity is an **EXPANSION**, not current revenue.

**Speaker note**

> Government is not the first wedge. Pet10x first earns household and building
> adoption. Once a region has meaningful coverage, it can offer municipalities
> a structured, jurisdiction-aware intake channel instead of asking residents
> to start over in another system.

**Suggested visual:** Today / Next split. Today ends at "official contact";

## Slide 13 — Veterinarians and hospitals

### The defensible veterinary wedge is trusted exchange—not replacing the clinic's software on day one

**What exists today — IN PRODUCT**

- Veterinary businesses can operate a storefront, publish services and hours,
  receive bookings, manage reviews, and appear in location-based discovery.
- Owners maintain vaccinations, documents, conditions, medication notes, vet
  visits, care logs, and emergency contacts.

**Act 1 — owner-controlled trust layer (EXPANSION)**

- Verified clinic identities, locations, and staff
- Owner-approved, scope-specific, expiring, revocable record sharing
- Every clinical read logged
- Vet-attested vaccination and treatment records published back to the owner
- Attested records can satisfy building requirements without another upload
- Appointment, referral, recall, and emergency handoff loops

**Act 2 — optional clinical platform (LONG-TERM EXPANSION)**

- Scheduling, encounters, pharmacy, diagnostics, estimates/invoices, inventory,
  and outreach
- Built only after security, privacy, availability, retention, regulatory, and
  clinical-governance prerequisites are met

**Why clinics care**

- Reach owners already on the network
- Reduce repeated history collection
- Turn vaccination recalls into booked appointments
- Give owners portable, verified records without replacing the clinic's current
  practice-management system at adoption

**Speaker note**

> Incumbent practice systems own the clinic chart. Pet10x's unique asset is the
> other side of the relationship: the owner, the pet, and the building. The
> first product for clinics should connect those parties and create verified
> records. Full EMR capability is a possible destination, not the opening claim.

**Suggested visual:** Trust loop: Clinic attests → owner record → building

## Slide 20 — Go-to-market

### Land a building. Activate owners. Build the local supply side.

**Phase 1 — Design partners**

- Recruit 3–5 pet-permitting BC stratas or rental properties with recurring pet
  administration pain.
- Define a 60–90 day pilot with baseline measures: manager time, incomplete
  records, response time, open incidents, owner activation, and satisfaction.
- Use one portfolio property manager where possible to prove multi-building
  deployment.

**Phase 2 — Repeatable building sale**

- Package onboarding, resident communication, data import, rules, and training.
- Sell through strata/property-management firms and verified Park10x/Pass10x
  relationships.
- Publish measured outcomes and a standard implementation timeline.

**Phase 3 — Local marketplace density**

- Onboard providers around participating buildings, beginning with vets,
  groomers, walkers, trainers, and boarding.
- Offer verified listings and booking tools before paid promotion.

**Phase 4 — Institutional expansion**

- Launch one clinic attestation pilot.
- Pursue one municipal integration only after enough local coverage and a
  signed data-governance agreement exist.

**Speaker note**

> The key metric is not downloads. It is activated households per building,
> manager time saved, workflow completion, and building renewal. Consumer and
> provider growth should be measured as expansion inside acquired building
> networks.

**Suggested visual:** Four-step land-and-expand arrow.

## Appendix C — Short talk tracks by audience

### If speaking to a pet owner

> Pet10x keeps every pet's daily care, records, reminders, building rules,
> community, and nearby services together. You can use it on your own, and it
> becomes more useful when your building, clinic, or provider participates.

### If speaking to a strata council or building owner

> Pet10x is a pet-operations system: know the pets in the property, communicate
> requirements, collect evidence, apply a consistent process, handle
> accommodation carefully, and keep a record that can be explained later.

### If speaking to a property-management company

> Pet10x standardizes the same high-friction workflow across a portfolio. Your
> team sees the buildings needing attention, uses one process for rules and
> incidents, and can report to councils without rebuilding a spreadsheet every
> month.

### If speaking to a municipality

> Pet10x can become a structured, jurisdiction-aware resident intake channel
> for animal-control and licensing workflows. Today it records and routes the
> resident to the official channel; direct municipal integration is the next
> partnership step.

### If speaking to a veterinary clinic or hospital

> Pet10x already brings clinics owner discovery and bookings. The strategic
> next layer is owner-controlled record exchange and clinic-attested vaccines,
> so the same trusted record can serve the owner, referral clinic, and building
> without repeated uploads.

### If speaking to a shop or service provider

> Pet10x gives you a verified storefront and demand from nearby pet households.
> You control services, prices, hours, radius, bookings, promotions, and
> reviews—without receiving a building's resident list.
